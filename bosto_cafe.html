Our Story
"We wanted to build the kind of place where you come in for breakfast and stay for lunch — and nobody rushes you out."

Bosto started as a small corner cafe in New Cairo in 2019. We had twelve tables, one espresso machine, and a menu written on a chalkboard. Five years later the menu has grown, we've opened a second location in Sheikh Zayed, but the feeling hasn't changed.

Everything we serve is made fresh in our kitchen each morning. No frozen pastries, no pre-made sauces. Just real food, good coffee, and a space that feels like yours.

Nadia & Omar
